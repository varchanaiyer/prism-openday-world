PRISM open day

Directory

Five rooms, joined by doors. Walk onto a door mat to go through. Stand on a sign or a coloured strip and press SPACE to read.

The Lobby

Welcome desk, programme, directory, and doors to everything else.

The Poster Foyer

Four doors, one per PRISM track, each with a coloured runner on the floor. The signpost in the middle lists them.

Technical Safety posters

NeurIPS 2025 posters on interpretability, steering and the mechanics of alignment.

Risks and Evaluations posters

NeurIPS 2025 posters on red teaming, safety benchmarks, multilingual and medical evaluation, and confidence.

Frontier Risks posters

NeurIPS 2025 posters on agent security, reward hacking and safety degradation in deployed systems.

Governance posters

NeurIPS 2025 posters on regulation, deployment security and where the field should go.

The Auditorium

The main stage. Everyone in the seats shares one call for talks and the cohort showcase.

The Team Rooms

Twelve pods, one per PRISM team. Read the nameplate, then press SPACE to join the team call.

The Lounge

Coffee, sofas, the get-involved board, and a quiet reading room.

Tips

  • Arrow keys or WASD to walk. Walk up to someone and your cameras connect.
  • SPACE opens whatever the prompt at the bottom of the screen offers.
  • The auditorium puts everyone in the seats into one call, so mute when you are not speaking.
  • The quiet room in the lounge keeps microphones off.
